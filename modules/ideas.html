What happens when you post
  1. Your idea lands on the feed. Anyone in the workspace can read it, upvote, or request to collaborate.
  2. The team gets a nudge. People active in the same space see it on their next visit.
  3. You decide who joins. Collab requests come to you. Accept, decline, or counter with a chat.
  4. Status moves through review. Admins can move an idea from Open for collab to In review to Adopted as the work matures.
